❤️ Cardiac Arrest (AED)

1. Start CPR Immediately

  • If the person is unresponsive and not breathing, begin chest compressions while someone else fetches the AED (Automated External Defibrillator).

2. Using the AED

  • Turn it ON: Follow the voice prompts immediately.
  • Expose Chest: Remove clothing from the person's chest. Wipe it dry if necessary.
  • Apply Pads: Place one pad on the upper right chest and the other on the lower left side, below the armpit.

3. Analyzing and Shocking

  • Stand Clear: Ensure NO ONE is touching the victim while the AED analyzes the heart rhythm.
  • Deliver Shock: If the AED advises a shock, ensure everyone is clear and press the "Shock" button.
  • Resume CPR: Begin chest compressions immediately after the shock is delivered or if no shock is advised.
