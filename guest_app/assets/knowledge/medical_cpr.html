❤️ Hands-Only CPR

1. Check the Scene and the Person

  • Ensure Safety: Make sure the environment is safe for you and the victim.
  • Check Responsiveness: Tap their shoulder and shout, "Are you OK?"
  • Check Breathing: Look for chest rise. If they are not breathing normally, begin CPR immediately.

2. Position Your Hands

  • Kneel: Kneel beside the person's chest.
  • Hand Placement: Place the heel of one hand in the center of their chest (between the nipples). Place your other hand on top and interlock your fingers.

3. Push Hard and Fast

  • Depth: Push down at least 2 inches (5 cm).
  • Rate: Push at a rate of 100 to 120 compressions per minute (to the beat of "Stayin' Alive").
  • Recoil: Let the chest rise completely between each compression.
  • Do NOT stop: Continue until an AED arrives, help arrives, or the person starts breathing.
