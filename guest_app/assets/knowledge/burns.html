🔥 Thermal Burns

Never apply ice, butter, or ointments to a severe burn.

1. Stop the Burning Process

  • Remove the person from the heat source immediately.
  • If their clothing is on fire, have them STOP, DROP, and ROLL.

2. Cool the Burn

  • Hold the burned area under cool (not cold) running water or apply a cool, wet compress for 10 to 15 minutes.
  • Do NOT use ice. It can cause further tissue damage.

3. Protect the Burn

  • Remove rings or other tight items from the burned area quickly and gently, before it swells.
  • Cover the burn loosely with a sterile, non-fluffy bandage or clean cloth.
  • Do not break blisters.
