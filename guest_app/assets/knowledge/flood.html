🌊 Flood / Water Damage

1. Move to Higher Ground

  • If water is rising, immediately move to a higher floor.
  • Do not try to walk or swim through moving water. Even 6 inches of fast-moving water can knock you down.

2. Electrical Hazards

  • DANGER: Do not touch electrical equipment if you are wet or standing in water.
  • Turn off the main power switch if you can safely reach it WITHOUT stepping in water.

3. Await Rescue

  • Do not climb into a closed attic where you could become trapped. Go onto the roof only if necessary.
  • Signal for help.
