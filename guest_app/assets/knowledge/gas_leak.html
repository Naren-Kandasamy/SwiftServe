⚠️ Gas Leak / Chemical Hazard

1. Evacuate Immediately

  • Leave the area immediately. Do not stay to find the source.
  • Evacuate UPWIND of the smell.

2. Prevent Ignition (CRITICAL)

  • DO NOT use matches or lighters.
  • DO NOT turn on or off ANY light switches or electrical appliances. A tiny spark can cause an explosion.
  • DO NOT use elevators. Leave via the stairs.

3. Chemical Contact

  • If a chemical spills on your skin, immediately flush the area with copious amounts of water for at least 15 minutes.
  • Remove contaminated clothing while flushing.
  • If fumes are present, cover your mouth and nose with a damp cloth if possible and seek fresh air.
