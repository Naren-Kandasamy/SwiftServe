🌍 Earthquake

1. DROP, COVER, AND HOLD ON

  • DROP down onto your hands and knees. This prevents you from being knocked over.
  • COVER your head and neck under a sturdy table or desk. If none is available, get next to an interior wall. Stay away from glass or windows.
  • HOLD ON to your shelter until the shaking stops.

2. If You Are in Bed

  • Stay there and cover your head and neck with a pillow.
  • Do not try to move. Broken glass on the floor is a common cause of injury.

3. After the Shaking Stops

  • Expect aftershocks.
  • Check yourself for injuries.
  • Evacuate the building if you smell gas or see structural damage. Use stairs, NOT elevators.
