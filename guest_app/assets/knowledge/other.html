⚠️ General Emergency Protocol

1. Stay Calm

  • Take a deep breath. Panic leads to poor decision making.
  • Assess your surroundings for immediate dangers.

2. Await Instructions

  • Staff and emergency responders have been notified via this app.
  • Follow any instructions pushed to your device or announced over the PA system.

3. Keep Communication Lines Open

  • Keep your phone on and with you.
  • Do not tie up phone lines except to provide critical updates to emergency services.
