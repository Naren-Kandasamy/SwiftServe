🔥 Fire Emergency

IF YOU ARE IN IMMEDIATE DANGER, LEAVE IMMEDIATELY. DO NOT GATHER BELONGINGS.

1. Evacuation Procedures

  • Stay Low: Smoke rises. Crawl or stay as close to the floor as possible to breathe cleaner air.
  • Feel Doors: Before opening any door, feel the handle and the door itself with the back of your hand. If hot, DO NOT OPEN IT. Find another exit.
  • Use Stairs: NEVER use the elevator during a fire. Always use the stairs.

2. If You Are Trapped

  • Seal the Room: Close all doors between you and the fire. Block cracks under doors with wet towels or clothing to keep smoke out.
  • Signal for Help: Go to a window. Wave a brightly colored cloth or use a flashlight at night to signal responders.
  • Stay by the Window: Do not break the window unless absolutely necessary for air, as it can draw smoke into the room.

3. If Your Clothes Catch Fire

  • STOP: Do not run. Running fans the flames.
  • DROP: Drop to the ground immediately.
  • ROLL: Cover your face with your hands and roll back and forth to smother the flames.
