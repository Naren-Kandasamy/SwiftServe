🩸 Severe Bleeding & Wounds

1. Stop the Bleeding

  • Apply direct, firm pressure to the wound with a clean cloth, tissue, or piece of clothing.
  • Do not remove the cloth if blood soaks through. Add another layer on top and continue applying pressure.

2. Elevate

  • If the wound is on an arm or leg, raise it above the level of the heart if possible, while maintaining direct pressure.

3. Treat for Shock

  • Have the person lie down.
  • Elevate their legs slightly (unless you suspect a head, neck, or back injury, or broken leg).
  • Keep them warm with a coat or blanket. Do not give them anything to eat or drink.
